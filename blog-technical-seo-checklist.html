Technical SEO Checklist for Small Business Websites

Most ranking problems on small sites are technical, not content. Work through these checks in order.

1. Confirm Google can index your pages

Verify your site in Google Search Console, submit your sitemap and open the Pages report. Look at why pages are not indexed. Noindex tags, blocked resources and duplicate pages are the usual causes.

2. Check robots.txt and canonical tags

Make sure robots.txt does not block important pages or CSS and JavaScript files. Each page should have one canonical URL that points to the version you want ranked, with HTTPS and a consistent domain.

3. Fix speed and mobile usability

  • Run PageSpeed Insights and review Core Web Vitals in Search Console.
  • Compress images and use modern formats.
  • Remove unused scripts and fonts.
  • Test every key page on a real phone.

4. Write unique titles, descriptions and headings

Each page needs its own title and meta description, one clear H1, and headings that follow the topic. Put the main keyword and, for local businesses, the location naturally in the title.

5. Add structured data

Use schema markup such as Organization, Person, LocalBusiness or FAQPage where it matches real content on the page. Validate it with Google's Rich Results Test.

6. Strengthen internal links

Link related pages with descriptive anchor text and make sure no important page is more than a few clicks from the homepage. Fix broken links and redirect chains.

7. Monitor monthly

Check Search Console for new errors, falling impressions and manual actions. Technical SEO is maintenance, not a one-time task.
