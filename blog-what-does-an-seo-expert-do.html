What Does an SEO Expert Do, and When Should You Hire One?

An SEO expert helps your website show up in Google for the searches your customers make. Here is what that work involves.

What an SEO expert does

  • Technical work: checks that Google can crawl and index your site, and fixes speed, mobile and structure problems.
  • Keyword research: finds the searches your customers use and maps them to pages.
  • On-page optimisation: improves titles, headings, content and internal links.
  • Authority building: earns relevant backlinks through outreach and guest posting.
  • Tracking and reporting: measures progress with Google Search Console and Analytics.

When it makes sense to hire one

  • You have a working website but little organic traffic.
  • You depend on local customers who search on Google.
  • Your traffic dropped and you do not know why.
  • You are about to launch or redesign a site and want to avoid SEO mistakes.
  • You do not have time to learn and maintain SEO yourself.

When it may be too early

If you do not have a website, a clear offer or any way to handle enquiries, fix those first. SEO brings visitors, but it cannot fix a business that is not ready for them.

What to look for

  • Clear explanation of the plan, not just promises.
  • No guaranteed rankings. Nobody controls Google.
  • Access to your own Search Console and Analytics accounts.
  • Reporting on traffic and enquiries, not only keyword positions.
  • Honest timelines: SEO usually takes months, not days.

Questions to ask before hiring

  • What will you check first on my site?
  • How will we measure success?
  • What do you need from me?
  • What are the risks of the link building you plan to do?
