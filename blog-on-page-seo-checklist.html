On-Page SEO Checklist for Small Business Websites

On-page SEO means making each page clearly match what its visitors search for. Use this checklist for every important page.

1. Pick one main keyword per page

Choose a phrase real customers would type, such as a service plus your city. Each page should target its own keyword so your pages do not compete with each other.

2. Title tag

Keep it under about 60 characters, put the main keyword near the start and make it read naturally. Every page needs a different title.

3. Meta description

It does not directly affect rankings, but a clear description of about 150 characters can earn more clicks. Say what the page offers and why to choose you.

4. One H1 and logical headings

Use a single H1 that describes the page, then H2 and H3 headings for sections. Headings should help a reader skim, not stuff keywords.

5. Helpful, original content

  • Answer the question behind the search completely.
  • Write for people first and mention related terms naturally.
  • Add real details: services, locations, pricing guidance, examples.
  • Update old pages when information changes.

6. Clean URLs

Short, readable addresses such as /services/local-seo are better than long strings of numbers.

7. Image optimisation

Compress images, use descriptive file names and write alt text that describes what the image shows.

8. Internal links

Link to related pages using descriptive anchor text, so visitors and Google can move through your site easily.

9. Clear call to action

Tell visitors what to do next, whether that is calling, booking or filling a form. Rankings only matter if visits turn into enquiries.

10. Check mobile and speed

Most searches happen on phones. Test your page on a real device and run PageSpeed Insights.
