How to Set Up Google Search Console (Step by Step)

Google Search Console is free and shows how Google sees your site. Setting it up takes about 15 minutes.

Step 1: Open Search Console and add a property

Go to search.google.com/search-console and sign in with a Google account. Click the property menu and choose Add property.

Step 2: Choose Domain or URL prefix

  • Domain: covers every subdomain and protocol, but needs DNS verification.
  • URL prefix: covers one exact address, such as https://example.com/, and offers easier verification methods.

If you do not control your DNS, for example on a free hosting subdomain, choose URL prefix.

Step 3: Verify ownership

Google offers several methods. The two simplest are:

  • HTML file: download the file Google gives you and upload it to the root of your site, then click Verify.
  • HTML tag: paste the meta tag into the head of your homepage, then click Verify.

Do not delete the verification file or tag afterwards, or you will lose access.

Step 4: Submit your sitemap

Open Indexing, then Sitemaps. Enter the sitemap address, usually sitemap.xml, and click Submit. The status can take hours or days to update.

Step 5: Request indexing for key pages

Paste a page URL into the inspection bar at the top, wait for the result, then click Request indexing. Do this for your homepage and your most important pages.

Step 6: Know which reports to check

  • Performance: clicks, impressions, average position and the queries people used.
  • Pages: which pages are indexed and why others are not.
  • Core Web Vitals: speed and stability problems.
  • Manual actions and Security issues: should always be empty.

New properties need a day or two before data appears, so do not worry if reports look empty at first.
