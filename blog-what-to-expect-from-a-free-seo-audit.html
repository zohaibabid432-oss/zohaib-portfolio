What to Expect from a Free SEO Audit (and What It Won't Cover)

A free SEO audit is a useful first look at your site, but it is not a full audit. Knowing the difference saves you time.

What a free audit usually covers

  • A quick check of whether your pages are indexed by Google.
  • Obvious on-page issues such as missing or duplicate titles and headings.
  • Basic speed and mobile problems.
  • The few biggest issues limiting your visibility.

It is a surface-level review. The goal is to show you where the biggest problems are, so you can decide what to do next.

What it will not cover

  • A full crawl of every page with a detailed report.
  • Complete keyword research and competitor analysis.
  • A backlink profile review.
  • Fixing the problems that are found.
  • A step-by-step strategy and ongoing monitoring.

These take real time and are normally part of paid work. That is how a free review can stay free.

How to get the most from it

  • Give the full website address and your main business goal.
  • Share what you sell and where your customers are.
  • Mention any traffic drops or past SEO work.
  • Ask which two or three fixes would matter most.

Red flags in free audits

  • Automatic reports full of scary scores and no explanation.
  • Promises of guaranteed first-page rankings.
  • Pressure to buy immediately.
  • No mention of what the audit does not cover.

What happens next

After the review you can fix the issues yourself, or ask for a quote for a detailed audit and implementation. Either way, you leave with a clearer picture of your site.
