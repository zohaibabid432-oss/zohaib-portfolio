How to Get Your Website Shown in Google AI Overviews

By Zohaib Abid, SEO Expert in Bahawalpur, Pakistan · Updated Nov 8, 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer

Google says no special markup is needed for AI Overviews: a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet. Clear direct answers, helpful original content, good structure and sound technical SEO give your pages the best chance of being cited.

On this page
  1. What are Google AI Overviews?
  2. How do you make your content easy to cite?
  3. What technical conditions apply?
  4. What should you avoid?
  5. Frequently asked questions

What are Google AI Overviews?

AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries that Google shows for some searches, with links to the pages that support the answer. They draw on pages in Google's normal search index.

How do you make your content easy to cite?

  • Put a short, direct answer under the heading, ideally in the first 40 to 60 words.
  • Use headings written as the question people ask.
  • Use lists, steps and simple tables for process and comparison content.
  • Define terms in plain language.
  • Show who wrote it and why they are qualified.
  • Keep facts accurate and update pages when things change.

What technical conditions apply?

  • The page is indexed in Google Search.
  • It is eligible to show a snippet, so no nosnippet or noindex directive.
  • It can be crawled, including important resources.
  • It loads fast and works on mobile.

What should you avoid?

  • Mass-produced AI content with no original value.
  • Keyword stuffing and hidden text.
  • Chasing a trick. Google's guidance is that ordinary SEO best practices apply.

Frequently asked questions

Is there schema markup for AI Overviews?

No special markup is required. Structured data helps Google understand a page, but it is not a switch for AI Overviews.

Can I guarantee my page appears in an AI Overview?

No. Appearance depends on the search and on Google's systems, so focus on helpful, well-structured, indexed content.
